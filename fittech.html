FOUNDER

Built from experience.
Driven by fitness

Everyone has that fitness bug within them

The desire to become stronger, healthier and better
is already there. What often gets in the way is
complexity, inconsistency and a disconnected
experience.

After more than 15 years in technology and
cybersecurity, with much of my work focused on data
security and building systems people can trust, I
experienced the fitness industry from the other side
— as someone genuinely passionate about fitness.

I saw how fragmented the journey can become
across training, nutrition, progress, health data,
coaching and everything surrounding them.

FitBug comes from a simple ambition: bring that
journey together and make it easier to keep moving
forward.

Janani Kanth
Founder, FitBug
